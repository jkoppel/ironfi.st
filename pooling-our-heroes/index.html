  • Pooling our Heroes

A new version of Ironfist has been released with the ability to modify heroes in the hero pool. It contains four new scripting calls, “GetHeroInPool”, “GetHeroForHire”, “GetHeroName”, and “GetHeroOwner”. The scripting documentation has been updated accordingly.

We are also pleased to announce that development of the Cyborg faction is officially underway, as a collaboration between Darmani and Orzie. Updates are being posted to the forum thread.
